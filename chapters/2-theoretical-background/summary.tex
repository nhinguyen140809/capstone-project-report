\section{Summary}
\label{sec:bg-summary}

This chapter introduced the concepts the proposed framework builds on. From requirements engineering it takes the iterative process of elicitation, analysis, specification and validation, the quality characteristics of ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148, user stories with the INVEST criteria, and traceability. From the persona literature it takes the idea of concrete stakeholder representations and the requirement that they be based on data, which leads to the grounded personas of this work. From LLMs it uses role prompting, structured output and retrieval of real documents, while its design responds to their hallucination, persona bias and cost. Finally, it executes the process as a LangGraph state graph of specialized agents with human checkpoints, and evaluates the result with reference-based, diversity and rubric-based metrics. Chapter~\ref{chap:related-work} reviews how existing work combines these elements.
