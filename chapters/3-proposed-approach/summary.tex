\section{Summary}
\label{sec:pa-summary}
This chapter presented \sysname{} as a workflow of eleven agent roles, human checkpoints and one control node, executed as a state graph over shared artifacts. Its personas are grounded in stakeholder observations mined from real documents; they are interviewed one question at a time; and their answers are analyzed, specified as requirements, written as a backlog and validated. Rework loops, review checkpoints, intent routing and, beside them, an assistant for questions about the application make the process non-linear but bounded, while the artifact model with its integrity rules and versions keeps every requirement traceable to its evidence. Chapter~\ref{chap:requirements} now turns to the framework as a software product and analyzes the requirements of the system that implements it.
