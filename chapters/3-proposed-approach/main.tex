\chapter{Proposed Approach}
\label{chap:proposed-approach}
\textit{This chapter presents \sysname{}, the proposed persona-driven multi-agent framework. It first gives an overview of the workflow, then describes how the stakeholder personas are grounded in evidence, how they are interviewed, how the elicited requirements are analyzed, specified and validated, how the user steers the process, and finally the artifact model that keeps every requirement traceable to its source.}
