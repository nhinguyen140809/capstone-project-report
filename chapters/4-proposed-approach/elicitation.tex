\section{Persona-Based Elicitation Interviews}
\label{sec:pa-elicitation}

The interviewer and the persona agent alternate until the turn limit of the persona is reached, then the next persona is interviewed; when all personas are done, the workflow moves to the analyst. Each of the two agents receives only the transcript of the persona being interviewed, which only grows at its end, so that repeated calls reuse the provider's prompt cache.
