\section{Agent Roles}
\label{sec:pa-agent-roles}

Table~\ref{tab:pa-agent-roles} lists the eleven agent roles of the workflow and the artifact each one produces. A role is made of three parts: its versioned instructions (Appendix~\ref{apx:prompts}), its inputs, which are snapshots of the artifacts it needs, and the output format it fills. What a role may change is limited to the items it returns, which the system writes into the shared artifacts after checking them; the corpus builder is the only role that may also act outside the workspace, through its search tool. The model of each role is chosen by configuration, so different roles may run on different LLMs.

\begin{table}[H]
    \centering
    \caption{Agent roles of the workflow and the artifacts they produce.}
    \label{tab:pa-agent-roles}
    \begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{|l|X|X|}
        \hline
        \textbf{Agent role} & \textbf{Responsibility} & \textbf{Produces} \\
        \hline
        Planner & Turns the product idea into an elicitation plan: objectives, topics and stakeholder types & Elicitation plan \\
        Corpus builder & Chooses the real-world documents the personas are based on, from member uploads first and web search after & Research corpus (source documents) \\
        Stakeholder miner & Finds what each kind of stakeholder cares about in those documents, with a verbatim evidence quote & Stakeholder observations \\
        Persona generator & Creates the personas from the evidence found in those documents & Persona catalog \\
        Interviewer & Asks interview questions to one persona at a time & Interview questions \\
        Persona agent & Answers as one persona & Interview answers \\
        Analyst & Extracts and analyzes requirements from the transcripts & Raw and analyzed requirements, latent needs, assumptions, ambiguity issues, conflicts \\
        Requirement engineer & Writes the requirement specification & Requirement specification \\
        Backlog manager & Writes the product backlog as user stories & Product backlog \\
        Validator & Checks the outputs and reports issues & Validation report \\
        Intent router & Decides which node a message sent by the user should go to & Intent directive \\
        \hline
    \end{tabularx}
\end{table}
