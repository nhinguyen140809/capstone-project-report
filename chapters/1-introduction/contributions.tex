The main contributions of this work are as follows:
\begin{itemize}
    \item We propose \sysname{}, a persona-driven multi-agent framework that structures requirements engineering as a dynamic, non-linear formalization process, from a product idea to a requirement specification and a prioritized backlog.
    \item We construct the stakeholder personas from evidence: the framework synthesizes uploaded documents, trustworthy public sources and interview evidence into structured stakeholder goals, concerns and constraints, and keeps every persona and requirement traceable to its source evidence and to the decisions taken on it.
    \item We design a multi-perspective deliberation workflow incorporating persona-based elicitation, rework loops, human review checkpoints and intent routing, to support non-sequential requirement refinement.
    \item We build the framework as a web application with a user-friendly interface in which the user follows the agents, reviews and edits their artifacts, and navigates the trace graph.
    \item We evaluate the contribution of multiple stakeholder agents with different perspectives to requirements elicitation and refinement (RQ1). \updating{summary of the results.}
\end{itemize}
