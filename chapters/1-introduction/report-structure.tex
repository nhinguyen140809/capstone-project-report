\section{Report Structure}
\label{sec:report-structure}

This report contains eight chapters:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Chapter~\ref{chap:introduction}} introduces the motivation and the related work, states the problem, the objectives and the scope of the project, lists the contributions, and gives an overview of the technologies.
    \item \textbf{Chapter~\ref{chap:background}} presents the theoretical background: requirements engineering, personas, large language models, LLM-based multi-agent systems, and the evaluation of generated requirements.
    \item \textbf{Chapter~\ref{chap:proposed-approach}} describes the proposed framework \sysname{}: the agent workflow, the grounded personas, the deliberation mechanisms and the artifact model.
    \item \textbf{Chapter~\ref{chap:requirements}} analyzes the requirements of the system: the stakeholders, their needs and their journeys, the functional and non-functional requirements, and the use cases.
    \item \textbf{Chapter~\ref{chap:system-design}} presents the design of the system: the behavioral models, the architecture with the alternatives considered and its realization of the non-functional requirements, the database, the API, the user interface, and the traceability from the requirements to the design.
    \item \textbf{Chapter~\ref{chap:implementation}} describes the implementation, testing and deployment, and demonstrates the prototype on a real idea.
    \item \textbf{Chapter~\ref{chap:next-phase}} presents the plan of the next phase: the evaluation plan for RQ1, the development plan and the order of the work.
    \item \textbf{Chapter~\ref{chap:conclusion}} summarizes the work, its limitations and the lessons learned.
\end{itemize}
The appendices give the detailed review of related studies, the prompts of the agents, and the project management records: an overview of the work areas, the timeline and the implementation log, with the member assigned to each task.
