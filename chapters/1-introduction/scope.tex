\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{RE activities.} The system covers elicitation, analysis, specification and validation of requirements, and the derivation of a product backlog for Agile development. Requirements management after delivery (change control, release planning) is out of scope.
    \item \textbf{Input.} A natural-language product idea, optionally with documents uploaded by the project members. Existing code bases or legacy specifications are not used as input.
    \item \textbf{Stakeholders.} Stakeholders are simulated by LLM agents grounded in public documents; interviews with real stakeholders are not part of the workflow. A human user supervises the process at review checkpoints.
