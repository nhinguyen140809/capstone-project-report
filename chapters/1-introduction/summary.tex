\section{Summary}
\label{sec:intro-summary}
This chapter motivated the project by the cost of incomplete and ambiguous stakeholder intent in requirements engineering. The review of related work showed that LLM-based approaches automate many RE activities, simulate stakeholders and coordinate several agents, but rarely combine these capabilities in one stakeholder-centered, end-to-end process with personas grounded in real evidence. From this gap the chapter derived the problem, the research questions with RQ1 as the focus of this phase, the objectives, the scope and the contributions of the project, and gave an overview of the technologies used to build \sysname{}. Chapter~\ref{chap:background} now introduces the concepts on which the framework rests.
