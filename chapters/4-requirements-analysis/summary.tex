\section{Summary}
\label{sec:ra-summary}
This chapter analyzed the requirements of \sysname{}. From the needs of four stakeholder groups and four user journeys, it derived eighteen functional requirements prioritized with the MoSCoW scheme and fifteen non-functional requirements grouped by quality characteristic, each with its verification method and implementation status. Thirteen use cases realize all the functional requirements except one that is planned, and their specifications describe the normal, alternative and exception flows. Chapter~\ref{chap:system-design} designs the system that meets these requirements.
