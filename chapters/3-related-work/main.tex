\chapter{Related Work}
\label{chap:related-work}
\textit{Recent advances in large language models (LLMs) have motivated a growing body of research on requirements engineering (RE), synthetic stakeholder representation, and multi-agent systems. To position our work, we organize the literature into three complementary streams: (1) LLM-based support for requirements engineering, ranging from task-specific automation to multi-stage and end-to-end workflows; (2) LLM-based stakeholder representation and persona modeling; and (3) mechanisms for collaboration and coordination among multiple agents. This organization allows us to distinguish between what RE activities are automated, how stakeholder perspectives are represented, and how multiple agents or perspectives are combined.}
